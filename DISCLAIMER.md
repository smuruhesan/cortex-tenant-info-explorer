# Disclaimer & Internal Use

**Cortex TAC Tenant Info Explorer · Version 1.0.0.0**

About Info Explorer (GitLab) and Tenant Investigator (Grafana) are internal TAC tools intended to help authorized engineers locate tenant configuration details and investigate operational telemetry.

The tools are **read-only investigation aids**, not a replacement for official product documentation, approved operational procedures, incident response or an engineer's technical judgment.

Configuration from GitLab describes intended or recorded settings; Grafana describes observations available from regional metric sources. Information may be incomplete, delayed, unavailable, redacted or inconsistent. Diagnostic results must be evaluated against their supporting evidence. A missing metric does not prove a feature is disabled, and an observed CLCS identifier does not establish subscription health.

Output may contain confidential customer and internal infrastructure information. **Do not distribute reports, screenshots, code or raw evidence outside authorized company channels without approval.**

The repository and tools do not grant access to GitLab, Grafana or customer systems and must not be used to bypass authentication, RBAC, CSP, DLP or managed-browser restrictions.

**No formal security certification, company endorsement or service-level commitment is implied by distribution in this repository.** Refer to applicable internal policies and obtain the necessary approvals for any broader use.

See [Security & Usage](docs/SECURITY_AND_USAGE.md) and [Support](SUPPORT.md).
